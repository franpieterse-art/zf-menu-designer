ZF Menu Studio V11.2 - 30 FZ menu cover designs

Upload index.html and the entire covers folder to the ROOT of your GitHub repository. Keep the covers/ filenames and paths unchanged.

The first 30 design choices are individually cropped from the reference image supplied by the user. They are flat background pictures: text baked into those images is not separately editable. New text, prices, and photos placed on top ARE editable. Designs 31-100 retain the earlier layout engine.

Open index.html in a browser to test; internet is needed for the Wikimedia photo search.
